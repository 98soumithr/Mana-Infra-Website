ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

Building India's Critical Infrastructure

MANA Infrastructure Private Limited is a civil engineering and infrastructure company focused on delivering large-scale projects with precision, reliability, and efficiency. Established in 2012 and incorporated as a private limited company in 2025, the company has built a strong execution track record across highways, tunnels, irrigation systems, railways, and bridges.

Headquartered in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, with a corporate presence in Hyderabad, we operate across multiple states with a skilled workforce and an extensive equipment fleet. Our approach is rooted in trust, defined by quality, and driven by timely execution, ensuring every project is delivered to the highest standards of safety and performance.

HOW WE OPERATE

End-to-End Project Execution

At MANA, every project is executed with an end-to-end approach, from planning to completion — ensuring strict control over timelines, quality, and safety at every stage. Direct leadership involvement enables seamless communication across management, engineering, and site teams, allowing faster decisions, reducing delays, and ensuring consistent, uncompromised delivery.

Our Approach

  • Ensure strict adherence to quality standards and safety practices at every stage
  • Plan, monitor, and execute projects with disciplined control from start to completion
  • Provide proper accommodation, facilities, and support for workforce well-being
  • Maintain continuous coordination across teams for smooth on-site execution

  • Mobilize resources consistently to meet project demands without delays
